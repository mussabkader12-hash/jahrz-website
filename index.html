Our story

Five friends, one bake sale, too many leftover cookies.

JAHRZ Bake & Brew Co. started as a class project between five friends who kept noticing the same thing at every school event: good cookies, mediocre coffee, and nothing sold together. So we started baking one and brewing the other, and testing which pairs actually worked.

"We didn't set out to start a business — we just wanted the cookie and the coffee to actually go together for once. Everything else came from there."

Our product

Two products, one experience.

A thick-cut cookie built to hold up in coffee, and an iced coffee built to complement it — never sold as separate afterthoughts.

✓100% Halal

Every ingredient we use — from the dairy and eggs in our dough to the flavorings in our coffee — is sourced from halal-certified suppliers. No gelatin, no alcohol-based extracts, nothing on the menu that requires a second look.

Each cookie is cut and baked thicker than standard, so the center stays soft enough to dunk without falling apart. Each iced coffee is brewed and sweetened specifically to match the cookie it's paired with.

  • Halal certified — all ingredients, no exceptions.
  • Baked fresh — same-day baking, no frozen dough.
  • Paired by design — every cookie has one matching coffee.
  • Small batch — made to order, not mass produced.
How it's made

From dough to dunk.

1. Mixed fresh

Dough is mixed the morning it bakes, using halal-certified dairy and eggs.

2. Filled & topped

Each flavor gets its own filling and topping, built around one core ingredient.

3. Paired & served

Every cookie ships with its matching iced coffee, brewed to order.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Is everything actually halal-certified, or just halal-friendly?
Fully certified — every dairy, egg, and flavoring supplier we use is halal-certified. Nothing on the menu is a judgment call.
How far in advance do I need to order for an event?
At least 10 days for anything over 20 cookies. Smaller personal orders can sometimes be turned around faster — ask us directly.
Can I mix flavors in one order?
Yes — the cart lets you add any combination of cookies, lattes, and cupcakes to a single order.
Do you deliver, or is it pickup only?
Both — enter your address at checkout and we'll confirm delivery or pickup details directly with you.
